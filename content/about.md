Amherst Aviation Club is for Amherst and Five College students who are curious about flying and airplanes — no experience, major, or license required.

This site is a practical starting point: learn how flight training works, explore the FAA materials that pilots use, practice with a flight simulator, and discover real aircraft and aviation history.

**What you can find here:**

- Clear, beginner-friendly guides to learning to fly
- Official FAA references alongside community resources
- Flight-simulator setup and practice ideas
- Articles on private aircraft, military aircraft, and aviation history
- Books, videos, museums, and organizations worth exploring

Meeting details and a contact method will be announced when they are ready to share.
