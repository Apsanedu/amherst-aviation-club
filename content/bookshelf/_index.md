Our shelf is small on purpose — five books that actually get you airborne in knowledge.

<div class="do-card"><h3>2. Weather</h3><p>METARs, TAFs, fronts. Read Aviation Weather Handbook + brief every sim flight.</p></div>

### Recommended starter stack

- FAA Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (free)
- FAA Airplane Flying Handbook (free)
- Stick and Rudder — Wolfgang Langewiesche (library)
- Weather Flying — Robert Buck (chapters 1–3)
