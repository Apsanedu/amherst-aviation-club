You need **nothing** to start. When you're ready, buy in this order:

1. **Paper checklist + kneeboard** ($15) — flies with you everywhere
2. **Logbook** ($12) — first entry at club night
3. **Headset (used / entry)** — try club loaners first; David Clark H10-13.4 or budget Kore
4. **Flight bag** — any small duffel; leather look optional
5. **Home yoke + pedals** — club owns one set; buy only if you sim weekly

<div class="checklist">

**Club kit (shared, do not remove):** yoke, pedals, 2 loaner headsets, sectional + plotter, checklist binder. Sign out with Sim Captain.
</div>

Ask before buying — Thursday gear table lets you try everything.
