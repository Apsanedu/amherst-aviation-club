A discovery flight is a short introductory lesson with a certificated flight instructor (CFI), usually 30 to 45 minutes in the air. It is the cheapest way to find out whether you actually enjoy flying before committing to training.

### What happens

1. **Preflight briefing** — the instructor walks you around the airplane, checking fuel, oil, and controls.
2. **Takeoff and climb** — the instructor handles the busy parts while narrating what they are doing.
3. **Your turn** — in cruise flight you will typically try gentle turns, climbs, and descents on the controls.
4. **Debrief** — questions, a logbook entry, and honest advice on next steps.

### Before you book

- Ask the total price including fuel and instructor time, not just the Hobbs rate.
- Ask which airplane you will fly and whether you can sit left seat.
- Bring sunglasses, skip the heavy breakfast, and say something immediately if you feel queasy — instructors deal with it weekly.
