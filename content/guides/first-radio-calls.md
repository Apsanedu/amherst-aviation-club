Radio work feels intimidating until you realize it is mostly fill-in-the-blank. Controllers expect student pilots and would rather you speak slowly than stay silent.

### The four calls to learn first

1. **Initial call-up** — who you are, where you are, what you want: *"Westover Ground, Skyhawk 12345, south ramp, VFR to the north with information Bravo."*
2. **Readbacks** — always repeat back runway assignments, headings, and altitudes. Read back what matters, acknowledge the rest.
3. **Traffic pattern calls** — at non-towered fields, announce who, where, and intentions: *"Barnes traffic, Skyhawk 12345, left downwind runway 20, Barnes."*
4. **The magic words** — *"Student pilot"* gets you extra patience and slower speech. Use it.

### Practice free

Listen to [LiveATC](https://www.liveatc.net/) for your local field, write down the pattern of calls, then rehearse them aloud at [Sim Station]({{< relref "/sim-station" >}}) before ever keying a real mic.
