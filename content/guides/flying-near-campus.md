You do not need to travel far to be around airplanes. The Connecticut River valley has several general-aviation fields within easy reach of campus, mixing towered and non-towered operations.

### Fields to know

1. **Westover (KCEF)** — a large joint-use field nearby; great for seeing heavy traffic mix and touring with the club.
2. **Westfield-Barnes (KBAF)** — towered, busy GA airport and a classic first cross-country destination.
3. **Northampton (7B2)** — small non-towered field, ideal for watching pattern work and practicing radio calls from the ground.
4. **Orange (KORE)** — quiet rural airport, home to skydiving and glider activity worth seeing once.

### Make it a trip, not just a list

Check the [Flight Board]({{< relref "/flight-board" >}}) for organized airport tours. Visiting with the club gets you ramp access and CFI narration you will never get driving there alone. Always confirm details with the airport before visiting.
