A private pilot certificate in the Northeast typically runs **$12,000–$17,000** all-in: airplane rental, instructor time, written exam, checkride fee, headset, and study materials. Most students take 60–70 flight hours even though the FAA minimum is 40.

### Three ways to spend less

1. **Fly consistently** — two lessons a week beats two a month. Long gaps mean paying to relearn.
2. **Chair-fly and sim between lessons** — every procedure you rehearse free is Hobbs time you do not buy twice.
3. **Finish the written exam early** — studying on the ground is far cheaper than learning theory in the air.

### Where scholarships live

Apply early and broadly: EAA chapters, AOPA, Women in Aviation, OBAP, and local airport associations all fund student pilots. Deadlines cluster in winter and spring for summer flying.
