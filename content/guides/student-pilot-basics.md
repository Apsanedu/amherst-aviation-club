You do **not** need a student pilot certificate just to take flying lessons. Before flying solo, however, a student needs the appropriate certificate, medical qualification when required, instructor training, and instructor endorsements.

For an airplane student pilot certificate, the FAA says an applicant must be at least 16 and able to read, speak, write, and understand English. The certificate application is handled through IACRA or FAA Form 8710-1 with an authorized person such as a CFI, flight school representative, examiner, or Flight Standards District Office.

### The practical takeaway

- Start by talking with a CFI; do not rush paperwork before you know whether lessons are a good fit.
- Ask the CFI when to begin the student-pilot and medical process for your training plan.
- Treat medical questions as personal: use the FAA’s official medical-certification information and an Aviation Medical Examiner rather than relying on a club website or social media.
- Keep your training record and endorsements organized; your instructor will tell you what applies before solo and before tests.
