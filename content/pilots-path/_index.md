Think of this poster as your map. You don't need to decide to become a pilot — just follow the pins.

### 1. First taste (free / cheap)

- **Discovery flight** (~30–45 min with a CFI at a local field like KCEF / KBAF — placeholder). You handle the controls in cruise.
- **Chair-flying:** sit, touch-poster, say checklists out loud. Sounds silly, works brilliantly.
- **Sim first:** do Mission 1 in [Sim Station](/sim-station/) before you pay for Hobbs time.

### 2. Private Pilot License (PPL) in plain English

1. Medical (BasicMed / 3rd class) + student pilot certificate
2. ~40 hrs min (most do 60–70): ground school + flight lessons
3. Written exam → checkride → license

<div class="checklist">

**Rough costs (Northeast placeholder):** $12k–$17k total. Club tip: fly consistently 2×/week, chair-fly between lessons, and apply early for scholarships.
</div>

### 3. Careers to explore

Pilots, flight dispatch, ATC, A&P maintenance, aerospace engineering, aviation medicine, and aviation law all offer different ways to be part of aviation.

### 4. Scholarships & orgs

- EAA Young Eagles, AOPA, Women in Aviation, OBAP, Latino Pilots Assoc.
- Local EAA chapter rides + FAA WINGS seminars.
