Free, friendly, and the best way to learn before you burn avgas.

### Setup (15 min)

1. Install **FlightGear** (free, Win/Mac/Linux)
2. Pick aircraft: **Cessna 172P**
3. Airport: **KCEF (Westover)** — wide runways, easy pattern
4. Weather: clear, winds calm. Controls: keyboard ok, yoke better.

### Beginner missions

- **Mission 1:** Straight-and-level + gentle turns (10 min)
- **Mission 2:** Climbs/descents at 500 fpm, trim for hands-off
- **Mission 3:** Pattern: takeoff → crosswind → downwind → base → final → go-around
- **Mission 4:** Full-stop landing, checklist aloud

<div class="checklist">

**Club sim rules:** 15-min slots on busy nights · briefing before engine start · crash = pause, debrief, retry · log your landings in the paper log.
</div>

Bring a laptop + mouse. Club yoke + checklist binder stay at the desk.
